IMULink

Privacy Policy  ·  Effective date: May 2026

Overview

IMULink is a free, open-source Android application that streams IMU sensor data from your phone to a computer on the same local network using UDP. IMULink does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Data collected

IMULink does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Sensor data (sensor data is sent directly from your phone to your own computer — it never passes through any external server)

Sensor data streaming

When you press START STREAMING, IMULink reads sensor data from your phone's hardware (accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, and optionally gravity, linear acceleration, and rotation vector) and sends it as UDP packets directly to the IP address and port you configure in Settings.

This data transmission occurs entirely within your local Wi-Fi network. IMULink has no servers, no backend, and no internet connectivity is required or used during streaming.

Local storage

IMULink stores the following settings locally on your device using Android's DataStore (a local key-value store):

  • Receiver IP address and UDP port
  • Sample rate preference
  • Sensor selection preferences
  • Onboarding completion flag

This data never leaves your device and is only used to restore your preferences between app sessions.

Permissions

IMULink requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET — required to send UDP packets to your computer
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE / ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — to check Wi-Fi connectivity and display your device's IP address
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC — to keep streaming active when the screen is off
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show the streaming status notification with a STOP button

No permission is used to collect or transmit personal data.

Third-party services

IMULink does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or crash reporting tools. The app contains no ads and makes no in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

IMULink does not collect any data from any user, including children. The app is intended for scientific and research use and is appropriate for all ages.
